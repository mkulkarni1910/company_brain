Full-time employees accrue 20 days of paid time off per year, prorated by start date.1

Requests go through the HR portal at least two weeks ahead for absences longer than two consecutive days. Unused PTO carries over up to 5 days into the next year, and is paid out at separation where required by law.1 The policy is owned by People Operations, last updated 2026-01-15.1
